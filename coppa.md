## 1. OVERVIEW

Stronger Kiddos LLC (“Stronger Kiddos”) complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (“COPPA”), 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506, and the FTC’s COPPA Rule, 16 C.F.R. Part 312. This COPPA Notice describes how we collect, use, and protect information about children under the age of thirteen (13) in the Stronger Kiddos mobile app.

## 2. OPERATOR INFORMATION

Stronger Kiddos LLC

Email: coppa@strongerkiddos.com

## 3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT ABOUT CHILDREN

We collect the following Child Data, provided by parents or Authorized Caregivers (not directly by children):

- Child’s name

- Date of birth

- Age

- Gender

- Child’s photo (optional)

- Parent-reported developmental progress: milestone check-ins, session feedback, and free-text developmental observations that you choose to enter about your child.

- Program activity data: program enrollment, exercise and session completion, video watch progress, position-hold results, and the reasons you give when ending an exercise early. This information is linked to your child’s profile so the app can show you their progress.

We maintain a per-child activity record (the items above) for the sole purpose of showing you your child’s progress and improving the quality and safety of our programs. We do NOT collect medical or biometric data, we do NOT use this information for behavioral, targeted, or interest-based advertising, and we do NOT build advertising or marketing profiles about your child. We do NOT use Child Data to make any automated medical, diagnostic, or treatment decision.

The above Child Data is sent only to our own backend service (operated by us on a Laravel/Sanctum platform) to operate the app. It is NOT sent to our analytics provider.

Separately, the app uses an anonymous Firebase Analytics (a Google service) app-instance identifier. This identifier is collected solely for the following internal operations: measuring screen and feature usage, counting sessions, monitoring app stability and crash-free sessions, and product-improvement analytics. It is not linked to your child’s name, profile, or account. We ensure this identifier is not used to contact any individual, for advertising, or to build a profile through the following technical means: the advertising identifier is not collected (the Android Advertising ID permission is removed and the app runs without the iOS IDFA); Google Consent Mode denies all advertising-related consent (ad storage, ad user data, and ad personalization); no per-user identifier is ever set; and an allowlist filter blocks any field that could identify a child before data leaves the device. The app shows no third-party advertising and collects no location data.

The app also collects a push notification token (provided by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) and your device platform in order to deliver app notifications. This token is associated with the parent account, is not Child Data, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

## 4. HOW WE USE CHILD DATA

Child Data is used solely to:

- Enable program selection based on the child’s age range;

- Provide content associated with the selected program;

- Show you your child’s progress in the app (milestone check-ins, completed exercises and sessions, and video watch progress that you and your child generate); and

- Improve the quality and safety of our Services.

We do NOT use Child Data for behavioral advertising, targeted advertising, interest-based advertising, or marketing profiling.

We collect only the Child Data needed for each feature, and we do not require optional information (such as your child’s photo) to use the core program. We never condition your child’s participation on disclosing more personal information than is reasonably necessary to use that activity.

## 5. HOW WE OBTAIN PARENTAL CONSENT

Email-Based Consent: Before any Child Data is collected or stored, we obtain verifiable parental consent through an email verification step. The parent creates an account, and we confirm the account through a verification message (a one-time code) sent to the parent’s email address. Child Data is not collected or stored until this email verification is completed.

Payment-Based Consent (when paid levels become available): Level 1 programs are free. When paid program levels (Level 2 and above) become available, completion of a purchase through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store in-app purchase will serve as an additional method of verifiable parental consent for those accounts.

## 6. DISCLOSURE OF CHILD DATA

We do not disclose Child Data to third parties except to service providers who are contractually prohibited from using the data for any purpose other than providing services to Stronger Kiddos, and who are required to protect it to the same or an equal standard as we do.

The categories of third parties involved in operating the app, and the purpose of each, are:

- Google (Firebase Analytics) — anonymous product analytics and internal operations only; receives NO Child Data.

- Google (Firebase Crashlytics) — crash diagnostics; any child identifiers are redacted before any report is sent.

- Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — delivery of push notifications; receives a parent-account device token, not Child Data.

- Our own backend service (Laravel/Sanctum), operated by Stronger Kiddos — the sole destination for child profile and progress data.

We do not sell or share the personal information of children, and we do not sell or share the personal information of any consumer under the age of sixteen (16). We treat children’s developmental data as sensitive personal information and do not use it beyond providing and improving the Services.

## 7. PARENTAL RIGHTS

Parents and Authorized Caregivers may at any time:

- Review the Child Data we have collected;

- Request correction or update of Child Data;

- Request deletion of Child Data;

- Refuse further collection or use of Child Data; and

- Request that Child Data not be disclosed to third parties.

To exercise these rights, contact coppa@strongerkiddos.com. We will verify your identity before processing any request. Deletion requests will be processed within thirty (30) days.

In-App Deletion: You can also delete your account and all child profiles directly in the app at Settings › Account Settings › Delete Account. Deletion takes effect immediately in the app and is completed on our systems within thirty (30) days, with backup copies purged within ninety (90) days.

## 8. DATA SECURITY AND RETENTION

We implement commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect Child Data.

We retain Child Data only as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected. Those purposes are to operate the app and to show you your child’s progress; the business need for retaining the data is the servicing of your active account. Child Data is retained for as long as the parent’s account is active. Children’s personal information is not retained indefinitely. Upon a deletion request or account termination, Child Data is deleted from active systems within thirty (30) days and from backups within ninety (90) days.
