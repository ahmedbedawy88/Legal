# Delete Your Account & Data

Stronger Kiddos mobile app · Stronger Kiddos LLC · Last updated: June 28, 2026

This page explains how to delete your **Stronger Kiddos** account and all data associated with it, including every child profile you created. You do not need to be signed in to read this page or to submit a deletion request by email.

## Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

- Open the Stronger Kiddos app.
- Go to **Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account**.
- Confirm. Your account and all associated data are removed immediately from the app.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email (no app required)

Email privacy@strongerkiddos.com (for child-data requests, coppa@strongerkiddos.com) from the email address on your account, with the subject line "Delete My Account." We will verify your identity and process the deletion.

## What is deleted

- Your parent account details (name, email, phone if provided, profile photo).
- All child profiles you created (name, date of birth, age, gender, and any photo).
- All parent-reported developmental progress and program activity tied to those profiles.
- Any photos you uploaded.

## Timing & retention

Deletion takes effect immediately in the app. Your data is removed from our active systems within **thirty (30) days**, and any backup copies are permanently purged within **ninety (90) days**. We may retain a limited set of records for longer only where required by law (for example, tax or fraud-prevention records). Anonymous, non-identifying analytics that are never linked to your account or your child are not affected.

## Questions

For any account- or data-deletion question, contact privacy@strongerkiddos.com. For child-data (COPPA) requests, contact coppa@strongerkiddos.com.
