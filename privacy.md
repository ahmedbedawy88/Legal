## 8. MOBILE APP DATA TECHNOLOGIES

The mobile app is a native application and does not use cookies, web beacons, or pixel tags. Instead, the app relies on device identifiers and software development kits (SDKs) to deliver and improve the Services, as described below.

### 8.1 Analytics and Diagnostics

Our mobile application uses a crash-reporting and performance-monitoring SDK to keep the app stable, and Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage analytics (such as screens viewed and features used) for our internal operations to improve the app. This analytics data is anonymous: it is not used for advertising, is not linked to your child or your account, no advertising identifier is collected, and we configure the analytics SDK to deny all advertising-related consent signals. We never set a user identifier, and every analytics event is filtered through an allowlist so that no parent or child name, email, or identifier is collected. Crash and performance diagnostics may include an anonymous install identifier, your device model and operating system version, and the technical details of an error; child and parent identifiers and email addresses are redacted before any crash report is sent. The app does not use any advertising SDK, does not request the iOS App Tracking Transparency permission, and performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking, so it does not appear under iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, or the Android Advertising ID, which is stripped from the app) for advertising, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers. You can manage app notifications and reminders within the app’s own settings.

### 8.2 Push Notifications

The app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications and reminders. To do this, a push token is registered for your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device’s operating-system settings, and notification content does not include sensitive child data.

### 8.3 Sign-In, Security, and Configuration

The app uses Firebase Authentication to support Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple; Firebase App Check to protect against abuse; and Firebase Remote Config to manage feature settings. These services do not collect advertising identifiers and are not used for advertising or tracking.
